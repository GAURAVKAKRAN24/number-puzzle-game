# Privacy Policy: Number Merge Puzzle

**Effective Date**: October 10, 2026  
**Last Updated**: October 10, 2026

## 1. Introduction
"Number Merge Puzzle" (the "Game") is developed as an offline-first, minimalist mobile puzzle game for Android. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Accounts**: You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or passwords.
- **No Cloud Database**: The game has no custom backend servers. All game scores, board states, and settings are stored locally on your device in the private sandbox directory (`user://`).
- **No Sensitive Device Permissions**: The game does **not** request or access:
  - Precise or coarse location
  - Camera or microphone
  - Contacts, address book, or calendar
  - Photos, media files, or external shared storage
  - SMS or phone call logs

## 3. Permissions Requested
The game requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary to function:
1. `android.permission.INTERNET`: Used strictly to load advertisement content via Google AdMob and check network connectivity.
2. `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Used by the Google AdMob SDK to determine whether an active internet connection is available before attempting to load an ad.
3. `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback during tile selection and merges (can be toggled OFF in Settings).

## 4. Third-Party Services and Advertising
The Game uses **Google AdMob** (a service provided by Google LLC) to display advertisements.

### Data Collected by Google AdMob
In accordance with Google's Mobile Ads SDK policies, AdMob may collect and process certain device and usage data for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and analytics, including:
- **Device Identifiers**: IP address, Android Advertising ID (GAID), and device hardware specifications (model, OS version).
- **Ad Interaction Data**: Ad views, impressions, clicks, and video completion status.
- **Diagnostics**: Crash reports and ad load latency.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 5. Google Play Store "Data Safety" Form Responses
When completing the Google Play Console Data Safety questionnaire, declare the following:

| Category | Data Type | Collected | Shared | Purpose | Ephemeral / Encrypted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Location** | Coarse / Precise | No | No | N/A | N/A |
| **Personal Info** | Name, Email, Phone | No | No | N/A | N/A |
| **Device or other IDs** | Advertising ID | Yes (by AdMob SDK) | Yes (with AdMob) | Advertising, Analytics, Fraud prevention | Encrypted in transit |
| **App activity** | App interactions | Yes (by AdMob SDK) | Yes (with AdMob) | Advertising | Encrypted in transit |
| **App info and performance** | Crash logs / Diagnostics | Yes (by AdMob SDK) | Yes (with AdMob) | Diagnostics | Encrypted in transit |

- **Security Practices**:
  - Data is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS.
  - Users can delete all locally saved game data at any time via the in-game Settings screen ("Reset All Saved Data").

## 6. Children's Privacy
Number Merge Puzzle does not target children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks through this app, you can reset or opt out of personalized ads via your Android device settings (**Settings -> Google -> Ads -> Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**).
